Telephone, played over a 1980s modem.

Every drawing and guess hops between phones as a modem screech, and the bad old line adds static on the way. No internet. Only sound.

  • 2–8 players, one phone or laptop each, sitting in seat order
  • Allow the microphone, volume up (iPhone: silent mode off)
  • Devices within about 1 m. Quiet while a chirp plays
  • Missed one? Ask for it by sound. Nothing breaks
