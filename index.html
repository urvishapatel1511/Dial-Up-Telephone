Ghost Frequency

Rescue a ghost together. It travels only by sound.

The ghost's chirps are how the game sends data between your devices, so they always play. This switch only turns off the decorative effects.

Diagnostics
Microphone off

Wrong answers cost a candle. Missed or garbled chirps never do: just resend.

Long before Wi-Fi, computers talked by singing to each other over phone lines: the acoustic modem. Ghost Frequency brings it back from the dead. Your ghost travels between two devices as a chirp, and you must work together to set it free.

Before you start

  1. Two devices, two roles. The House (best on a laptop) draws the ghost. The Other Side (best on a phone) is the haunted room. Each of you sees clues the other can't, so talk out loud!
  2. Allow the microphone when asked. Sound is processed only on your device.
  3. Volume up on both devices. On iPhone, turn off silent mode.
  4. Place the devices 30–50 cm apart, speakers facing each other. Keep the room fairly quiet while a chirp plays.
  5. Three candles. Each wrong answer puts one out. Sound problems never do: if a chirp is missed, press Resend.

Who are you on this device?

The other player picks the other role on their device. Only one device? Open this page in two browser windows and pick one role in each.
